Certification exam

Before you begin.

Six sections, about half an hour. Several questions are written to catch the difference between a fact you memorized and a fact you understood.

One request, and we mean it

Write the long-form answers in your own words. We are not scoring polish, we are scoring judgement. An answer that says "I would not claim that, here is what I would say instead" scores higher than a fluent answer that oversells the product.

What you may have open

Nothing. Close the curriculum. The whole point is to find out what you carry into a live call, and a buyer will not wait while you look something up.

Final step

Submit for certification.

Your answers, your signed NDA record and your elapsed time go to PrivacyPal operations in one submission. You will not see your own score: a person reviews it and comes back to you.
