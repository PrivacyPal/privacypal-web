How it works

Detect, swap, restore. On your device.

Protection runs on your device, before a prompt or file reaches an AI provider. Sensitive details are swapped for Privacy Twins, realistic stand-ins, so the AI still works and the real data never reaches the AI provider.

  1. 01

    Detect on your device

    The moment you type it, PrivacyPal finds the names, numbers and secrets in your prompt and the files you attach.

    You type

    Draft a note to Dana Whitfield confirming her $48,200 wire to account 0047 8821 9360.

  2. 02

    Swap for Privacy Twins

    Each detail is replaced by a realistic stand-in, so the AI has everything it needs to help. Only the twins reach the AI provider.

    The AI receives

    Draft a note to Grace Holloway confirming her $51,700 wire to account 0093 4586 2737.

  3. 03

    Restore in the reply

    When the answer comes back, PrivacyPal puts the real values back on your device. Same answer, your real details.

    You read

    Hi Dana, your $48,200 wire to account 0047 8821 9360 is confirmed and on its way.
