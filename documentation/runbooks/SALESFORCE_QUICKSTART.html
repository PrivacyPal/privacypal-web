Before you start

  • System Administrator access to the Salesforce org you want to protect.
  • PrivacyPal admin access: Connections is admin-only.
  • A few minutes of patience: Salesforce takes 2–10 min to activate a new Connected App.
Part A · In Salesforce

Create your Connected App

You do this once, inside your own Salesforce org. It produces the Consumer Key & Secret PrivacyPal will use.

1

Confirm your My Domain URL

Go to Setup → Company Settings → My Domain. Copy your login URL. You'll paste it into PrivacyPal. Sandboxes use their own …sandbox.my.salesforce.com address.

2

Register a new Connected App

In Setup → App Manager, choose New Connected App. Name it PrivacyPal, add your email, then enable OAuth and fill in exactly these two things:

  • Callback URL: paste the one PrivacyPal shows in Step 5. It's the same for everyone:
https://api.privacypal.ai/api/business-data/connections/oauth/salesforce/callback
  • Selected OAuth Scopes: add Manage user data via APIs (api) and Perform requests at any time (refresh_token, offline_access).
Give it a moment. After you Save, Salesforce needs 2–10 minutes to activate the app. Connecting too soon shows an "OAuth not active" / app-not-enabled error. Just wait and retry.
3

Copy the Consumer Key & Secret

Back in App Manager, open the PrivacyPal app → View → Manage Consumer Details (Salesforce may re-verify your identity). Copy both values. You'll paste them into PrivacyPal next.

Who can connect & what gets read. The scan reads as the Salesforce user who approves in Step 6, so use an admin or integration user with read access to Contacts, Leads, Accounts, Cases and Opportunities. If your org blocks unapproved apps, set the app's Permitted Users policy and, if needed, relax IP restrictions.
Part B · In PrivacyPal

Connect the org

Now hand PrivacyPal your app details and approve access. Tokens are exchanged server-side and stored encrypted.

4

Open the Salesforce connector

In PrivacyPal, go to Connections → Add connection → CRM & Business Systems → Salesforce.

5

Enter your app details

On the Authenticate step, PrivacyPal shows the exact Callback URL to register (that's the value from Step 2; copy it there if you haven't yet). Then paste your My Domain login URL, Consumer Key, and Consumer Secret, and press Connect with Salesforce.

6

Approve access

A Salesforce window opens. Log in if prompted and choose Allow. The window closes, PrivacyPal exchanges the code for tokens, and the connection flips to Active.

Part C · In PrivacyPal

Index & verify

Build the sensitive-data catalog from your org, then confirm protection is live.

7

Scan to build your catalog

On the Configure step you can list any Object.FieldApiName to exclude (leave it blank to scan all relevant fields), then finish the wizard. On the Salesforce connection card, press Scan (or Sync All to run every connection).

PrivacyPal enumerates Contacts, Leads, Accounts, Cases, Opportunities and any relevant custom objects, then catalogs each sensitive value as a salted hash, never plaintext.

8

Confirm your catalog & protection

When the scan finishes, the Business Data Catalog fills with indexed items grouped by category. From here on, any matching value that appears in an AI prompt is automatically protected. No further action needed.

That's it. The connection stays fresh on its own: tokens refresh automatically and the daily rebuild re-scans every active org. You only revisit this if you rotate the Connected App's secret or want to reconnect.
Reference

If something goes wrong

"OAuth not active" / app not enabled
The Connected App was just created or edited. Wait 2–10 minutes for Salesforce to activate it, then retry the connection.
redirect_uri_mismatch
The Callback URL in your Connected App doesn't match. Copy it again from the wizard; it must match exactly, including scheme and path.
"Invalid Salesforce host" before the popup
Your login URL isn't a recognized Salesforce host. Use your …my.salesforce.com (or sandbox) URL, not a custom CNAME.
Popup closed / "authentication cancelled"
Allow pop-ups for PrivacyPal and try again. The half-finished connection is cleaned up automatically.
Connects, but the scan finds little data
The approving Salesforce user lacks read access. Reconnect as a user with read permission on the target objects.

Tenant-isolated by design. Every customer connects with their own Connected App; PrivacyPal makes server-side calls only to verified Salesforce hosts, encrypts your secret and tokens per-tenant, and stores catalog values as hashes. Revoke anytime from Salesforce → Connected Apps OAuth Usage, or by removing the connection in PrivacyPal.
