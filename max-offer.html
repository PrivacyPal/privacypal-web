questions buyers ask

Straight answers

Does this block the AI tools people already use?
No, and that is the whole design. Blocking moves the work to personal laptops where you cannot see it. People keep ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity. What changes is that the sensitive value is swapped for a Privacy Twin before the request leaves the device.
How long does a rollout actually take?
An afternoon for the install, and policy from there. Attach the domain, send one installer, and people sign in with their work account. There is no infrastructure to stand up.
Can we evaluate before we buy?
Yes. Self-serve Max is paid from the first seat, but sales can issue a company-wide evaluation against a trial code. Some trial codes run as a normal subscription with a card on file; others are enterprise evaluations that run for a fixed number of days with no card at all. The code says which, and this page tells you before you commit.
What does the seat price do as we grow?
It falls, automatically, with the band you land in. From $30 per seat a month at the entry band down to $17 from 1,000 seats, billed annually. You do not renegotiate to get there.
Where does our data actually go?
The sensitive parts do not leave the device. Detection and substitution run locally, and the mapping back is held on your side, so the model provider only ever sees the twin.
