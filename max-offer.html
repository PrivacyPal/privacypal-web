Questions buyers ask

Straight answers.

Do people keep the AI tools they already use?
Yes, and that is the whole design. People keep ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity, plus the agents they run. What changes is that the sensitive value is swapped for a Privacy Twin before the request leaves the device. Banning the tools moves the work to personal laptops; governing them keeps it in view.
How long does a rollout actually take?
An afternoon for the install, and policy from there. Attach the domain, send one installer, and people sign in with their work account. There is no infrastructure to stand up.
Can we see it working before we buy?
Yes. Book a demo and we will show PrivacyPal protecting a real prompt in the tools your people already use. Anyone can also download PrivacyPal and use Personal, free, on their own Mac or Windows computer. The Max pack is paid from the first user, and for a larger rollout our sales team plans it with you.
What does the price do as we grow?
It falls, automatically, with the band you land in: from $30 per user a month at the entry band down to $17 from 1,000 users, billed annually ($34 down to $22 billed monthly). You do not renegotiate to get there.
What do our people get on their own?
Everyone who downloads PrivacyPal has a Personal account, free. You invite them into your company's Max account under the license you provision, and the company layer switches on in the same app, on the same PrivacyPal account. Anyone who holds the Family pack for their kids keeps it: the company seat stacks with it.
Where does our data actually go?
Protection runs on the device, before a prompt or file reaches an AI provider. Detection and substitution run locally, and the twins are swapped back to the real values in the answer on the same device, so the model provider only ever sees the twin.
