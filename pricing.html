Account scope

Pro protects a person. Max protects an organization.

The line between the two plans is not only a feature list. It is who the account belongs to.

Pro · individual

One seat. One person. That is the whole design.

  • A single named individual. Your login, your devices, your seat. Not a team seat, not a shared seat, not a seat that travels between people.
  • No domain accounts. You cannot attach a company domain to Pro or provision colleagues under it.
  • No volume pricing. One seat is one seat, at one price. Buying nine of them does not make them cheaper.
  • Everything an individual needs. Real-time interception across every AI app on the machine, PII, HIPAA, PCI and GDPR coverage, 5-day free trial, cancel anytime.
Max · teams and organizations

Built for the second seat, and the two-thousandth.

  • Domain accounts. Attach your company domain, provision and de-provision centrally, keep one policy plane across the org.
  • Volume pricing built in. Four tiers from 1-9 up to 1000+, applied automatically as the rollout grows.
  • One domain, one plan. Any company domain with more than one user runs on Max, across every user on it. Pro cannot attach to a company domain at all.
  • Everything an organization needs. Connections, on-device DSPM, agent governance, Private MCP, Privacy Agents and org-wide AI policy.
