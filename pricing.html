Can I have Family and Max together?

Yes. Packs stack in the one PrivacyPal app. Your company can provide Max for work while you add Family for your kids, and both run side by side.

A parent can also add Max on top of Family on their own, to protect their work in Claude Code, Microsoft Copilot and every other Max surface. Each pack is paid for by whoever provides it.

What does Personal cover?

Personal is free with every download: no card, no trial clock, no expiry. It protects you on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity web chat, attachments included. Names, SSNs, account numbers, patient details and cardholder data are swapped for Privacy Twins on your device before a prompt reaches the AI.

It runs on Mac and Windows, and your usage view shows the last 24 hours. Personal is an individual account: your login, your devices.

Can my company add Max to my PrivacyPal account?

Yes. Your company provisions Max licenses and invites you into its Max account. Accept the invitation and your company's protection and policies switch on in the PrivacyPal app you already use. If you hold a Family pack, it keeps running alongside Max.

New to PrivacyPal? Download it and sign in with your work email. Your IT team may also send you the installer directly.

Which packs include phones?

Phones come with the Family and Max packs. PrivacyPal protects iPhone, iPad and Android today with Family or Max, and you install it by invitation. Mac and Windows come with every pack, and Max adds the Linux headless agent for servers and WSL.

With Family, Parent HQ runs on your phone too: the feed, guardrails, alerts and the never-share list. See every platform.

How does Max volume pricing work?

Max is priced per user per month. The price per user drops at 10, 100 and 1,000 users, and the tier your user count falls in sets the price for every user. Tiers apply automatically at checkout and at renewal.

Annual billing saves 12% at 1–9 users, rising to 23% at 1000+. Try the user slider.

How do I try PrivacyPal before buying Max?

Start on Personal: download PrivacyPal, free, and feel the engine on your own chats today. Max is paid from day one and switches on the moment your company needs protecting. For a larger rollout, book a demo and we will walk your team through it.

What do Cloud and the SDK cost?

PrivacyPal Cloud is a custom annual agreement, sized to your deployment. The SDK runs self-hosted against Cloud in your own VPC. Talk to sales and we will scope both with you.
