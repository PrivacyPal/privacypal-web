How it works
How it works

The moment you type it, we protect it.

Protection runs on the device, before a prompt or file is sent. Sensitive details are swapped for Privacy Twins, realistic stand-ins, so the AI still works and the real data never reaches the AI provider.

One prompt, start to finish Illustrative example
What you type

Draft a renewal letter for Maria Alvarez, policy 4471-2290, at 14 Harbor Lane.

PrivacyPal filter
What the AI receives

Draft a renewal letter for Dana Whitfield, policy 7730-5512, at 88 Cedar Court.

What you get back

Dear Maria Alvarez, thank you for renewing policy 4471-2290 for 14 Harbor Lane.

0 bytes of sensitive data sent to AI providers. The twins go out; your real values come back in, on your device.
