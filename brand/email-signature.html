Add it to your mail app

Press Copy signature first, then follow the steps for your app.

Gmail

  1. Settings, then See all settings.
  2. General tab, Signature, then Create new.
  3. Paste into the box.
  4. Save changes at the bottom of the page.

Outlook (new and web)

  1. Settings, Accounts, then Signatures.
  2. New signature and name it PrivacyPal.
  3. Paste into the editor.
  4. Pick it for new messages and replies, then Save.

Outlook for Windows (classic)

  1. File, Options, Mail, then Signatures.
  2. New, name it, and paste into the editor.
  3. Set it as the default for new messages and replies.
  4. Press OK.

Apple Mail (Mac)

  1. Mail, Settings, then Signatures.
  2. Choose your account and press +.
  3. Uncheck Always match my default message font.
  4. Paste into the right-hand box.

Phones and plain-text mail

iPhone and Android mail apps keep signatures as text. Paste this version there.
