Install

Three steps to protected

01 · Download

Pick your platform

Choose Mac or Windows above. On a Windows PC you can also run winget install PrivacyPal.AI from PowerShell. Linux servers on Max run the headless agent.

02 · Sign in

Open PrivacyPal and sign in

Personal is on from the first minute. A Family or Max pack on your account switches on in the same app.

03 · Protected

Use AI the way you already do

Protection runs on the device, before a prompt or file reaches an AI provider. Sensitive details become Privacy Twins, so the AI still works.

On a Mac

Open the .dmg and drag PrivacyPal to Applications. Approve the permissions it asks for: they let PrivacyPal protect every AI app on the Mac.

On Windows

Run the installer as a user who can install applications. PrivacyPal starts at sign-in, so protection is on every time you log in.

Before you install

Close other AI apps if the installer asks you to. PrivacyPal updates itself in the background after that.

Linux · the Max headless agent

For servers and WSL

The headless agent protects the command-line AI agents your engineers run: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes Agent and the OpenAI and xAI APIs. It comes with the Max pack.

  • x86_64 and aarch64 builds, the current agent release
  • Runs as your own user, with no desktop session
  • Tested on Ubuntu 24.04 and Fedora 40, and the same under WSL
  • Every tarball ships with a matching .sha256 checksum
x86_64 .tar.gz aarch64 .tar.gz

Checksums: x86_64 · aarch64

From the shell
01 · Download
$curl -LO
02 · Verify
$sha256sum -c privacypal-agent-*.tar.gz.sha256
03 · Extract and run
$tar -xzf privacypal-agent-*.tar.gz

Then start the agent from the extracted directory. It protects the command-line AI agents on that machine.

Questions

Before you download

Is PrivacyPal free?

Yes, for you. Personal is included free on Mac and Windows: no credit card, no trial clock, no expiry. It protects ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity, attachments included.

The Family pack is $9.99 a month per household, for up to 5 family members, with a 5-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. The Max pack is $34 per user per month, with volume pricing for larger teams. Compare packs.

Do the Family and Max packs need their own download?

One download covers every pack. Sign in, and the packs on your account switch on in the same app. Packs stack: you can hold the Max pack from work and the Family pack for your kids at the same time.

Can my company add Max to my PrivacyPal account?

Yes. Your company can invite you into its Max account under a license it provisions, whether you use Personal or hold the Family pack. Accept the invitation and sign in: Max switches on with your company’s policies, and your own Family pack keeps running alongside it.

Your IT team may also send you the installer directly. A partner license works the same way.

Which computers does PrivacyPal run on?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 and later) on macOS 13 or later, and Windows 11 PCs (64-bit), on every pack. Linux servers and WSL run the Max headless agent, on x86_64 or aarch64. See the system requirements.

Can I use PrivacyPal on iPhone, iPad and Android?

Yes, with the Family or Max pack. PrivacyPal protects iPhone, iPad and Android today, and you install it by invitation: request your invitation and it arrives by email, through Apple TestFlight or Google Play testing. Personal protects you on Mac and Windows.

How do accounts work for a family?

One PrivacyPal account for you. Your kids keep their own protected profiles in your Family pack household. If your company adds Max, it stacks on the same account, next to your Family pack.

How do I know the download is genuine?

The Mac app is notarized by Apple and the Windows installer comes from a trusted publisher. Every Linux tarball ships with a .sha256 checksum you can check with sha256sum -c before you extract it.

Where does the protection run?

On your device, before a prompt or file reaches an AI provider. Sensitive details are swapped for Privacy Twins, realistic stand-ins, so the AI still works and the real data never reaches the AI provider. How Privacy Twins work.
