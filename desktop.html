Before you install

Three things worth knowing

  • Close other AI apps if the installer prompts you. PrivacyPal hooks in at the system edge for full coverage.
  • Windows: run the installer as a user with permission to install applications; SmartScreen may ask you to confirm the publisher.
  • macOS: open the .dmg and drag PrivacyPal to your Applications folder. On first launch Gatekeeper may block the app. Right-click PrivacyPal in Applications and choose Open, then confirm to grant any privacy or accessibility permissions it requests.
