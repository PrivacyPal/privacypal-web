The SKU

What you sell on Monday

Two products. One governance plane.

One agent on the device. Privacy Twins replace names, account numbers, patient identifiers, source code and IP with statistically faithful stand-ins before the prompt leaves. The AI answers normally. The real data never went anywhere. 47 PII, PHI, PCI and IP classes governed out of the box; SSO; a full audit trail your client's auditor can read.

  • PrivacyPal Max, the partner SKU: the governance layer for the whole company, 20 to 2,000 people. PrivacyPal Cloud: the sovereign version in the client's own cloud, on-prem or air-gapped, co-sold and priced per deployment.
  • Mac, Windows, iOS and Android. Zero-touch through Intune or Jamf Pro, or personal phones (BYOD) where only AI traffic ever enters the tunnel. SSO through Okta, Microsoft Entra ID or Google Workspace.
  • Every major AI: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and Grok. Agents too: Claude Code, Copilot, Hermes and Private MCP. It isn't tied to one vendor's ecosystem.
  • Policies, shadow-AI discovery and on-device DSPM, plus Private Memory: one governed memory across every AI.
  • Multi-tenant console so you manage every client from one place.
  • Per-user AI usage and spend visibility: a second buyer (the CFO) off the same agent.

How you take this to your clients in the next 30 days

2

Ask three questions in the next QBR

"Which AI tools are your people using today?" "What's your current answer on the insurance form about AI?" "If I could show you what left the building last month, would you want to see it?" The third question is the pilot.

3

Offer the 30-day pilot on their regulated team

Fifteen to twenty percent of staff, the ones who touch client data. Same path you ran on your own team. The audit report at day 14 is the close.

4

Price it as a managed line, not a license

Seats at partner margin plus your monthly AI-governance service: policy by user group, quarterly report, AI spend attribution, insurance-renewal support. The license is the floor; the service is the margin.

5

Register, close, renew

Register the opportunity under your partner record on day one. Signed in weeks. Renews annually. Expand to the next department at the next QBR.

Messages you can send this week

Each one in the client's language, not ours. Change the vertical sentence to match the account.

Email, line one: "Your team is using AI whether we've approved it or not. I can show you, by user and by tool, what's gone out in the last 30 days, and then make it stop without blocking anyone. 20 minutes?"

LinkedIn post: "Banned AI at your firm? Your people didn't stop. They moved to their phones. There's a way to let them use it and keep client data home. Ask me how we're doing it for our clients."

QBR slide, one line: "AI data protection: the line item your insurer, your examiner and your staff are all asking for."

When they push back

  • "Purview does this." Purview inspects and blocks inside the Microsoft estate. This substitutes, so the AI still works, and it covers every AI tool and the phone. Run them together.
  • "We blocked AI." Then it moved to personal accounts and personal phones. The pilot report will show it.
  • "We're too small." Start with ten seats for the people who touch client data. That's the whole point of targeted population.
  • "What about SOC 2 and security review?" Ask us for the security packet; we send it the same day.

Rules of engagement

How registration, ownership and conflicts work

Registration

Register the deal through the portal; end-customer accounts register beneath your partner record. An approved deal carries 90 days of margin protection, extendable in writing.

Decisions

Registration decisions within two business days. No answer means approved.

Terms

Close at retail and remit retail minus your margin within 30 days. Non-exclusive, no territory lock-in. Tiers reviewed annually, with written notice and a 90-day cure period.

Named accounts

Submit your named-account list at onboarding. Those accounts are yours regardless of registration.

Collisions

A direct conversation that collides with a registered partner converts to partner-led. Conflicts escalate to the VP Channel and resolve in the registered partner's favor.

Customer ownership

You own the services relationship, the invoice for your managed line, and the renewal conversation. We don't call your client without you.

Claims

Partners quote published list prices and the program tier schedule. Product and compliance claims come from the fact sheet we give you, nothing else.
