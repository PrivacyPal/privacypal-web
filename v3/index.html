How it works

We govern your most sensitive data

LLMs are language. So we govern them with language. The moment sensitive data appears in any prompt, file or agent call, PrivacyPal substitutes a Privacy Twin — statistically accurate, context-preserving synthetic data. The AI gets a high-quality answer. Your IP never leaves the device.

01 — What you type
Hi ChatGPT, draft an email to Jane Thompson at Acme Corp about our upcoming $2,340,500 renewal — her SSN on file is 412-88-9120 and we should mention the Q4 expansion in Dallas.
02 — What the AI sees
Hi ChatGPT, draft an email to Evelyn Ward at Orion Mfg about our upcoming $2,112,800 renewal — her SSN on file is 603-14-7298 and we should mention the Q4 expansion in Frisco.
