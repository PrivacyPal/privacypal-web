What your review board will ask.

Where does the sensitive data actually go?

Nowhere. Detection and substitution happen on-device, before the prompt leaves the endpoint. The real-to-twin mapping is held locally and never transmitted, and the LLM only ever sees synthetic values. It's a zero-knowledge architecture — we can't see your data either.

How do we prove control to an auditor?

Every prompt, interception and swap is logged per-user and streamed to your SIEM. Shadow-AI discovery gives you visibility into which tools are in use across the org, and the evidence maps to SOC 2, ISO 27001, the NIST AI RMF and data-protection regimes like GDPR, HIPAA and PCI DSS.

Does it cover agents and coding assistants?

Yes. Beyond chat apps, PrivacyPal governs Claude Code, Microsoft Copilot agents and Private MCP calls — the agentic surfaces where data leaves without a human in the loop.

Can it run entirely inside our environment?

Yes. PrivacyPal Cloud is a self-hosted gateway that lives in your VPC — API-compatible with OpenAI, Anthropic and Google — for full data sovereignty, with an air-gapped deployment option and network DSPM scanning across your databases and data lakes.

How long does rollout take?

Days, not quarters. Install once and every AI surface on the device is governed — no model retraining, no workflow rebuild. Most teams go from evaluation to org-wide coverage in a matter of weeks.
