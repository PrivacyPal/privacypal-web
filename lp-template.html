Straight answers

What your team will ask.

Where does our data go?

Protection runs on the device, before a prompt or file reaches an AI provider. Sensitive details are swapped for Privacy Twins, realistic stand-ins, so the AI still works and the real values are restored in the answer on your device.

Which AI tools are covered?

Personal covers ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity web chat, attachments included. The Max pack adds Claude Code, Codex, Microsoft Copilot and Copilot agents, Hermes Agent and the OpenAI and xAI APIs.

Which devices does it run on?

Personal runs on Mac and Windows. iPhone, iPad and Android are protected today with the Family or Max pack, and install by invitation. The Max pack also adds the headless agent for Linux servers.

What does it cost?

Personal is free for every individual. The Max pack is $34 per user a month, or $30 billed annually, falling to $17 at volume. The Family pack is $9.99 a month per household. Your company can add Max to anyone's PrivacyPal account under a license it provisions, and it stacks with their own Family pack.

Can it run inside our own environment?

Yes. PrivacyPal Cloud is the sovereign deployment: the governance plane in your VPC, with an air-gapped option and custom annual pricing.
