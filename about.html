About PrivacyPal · the mission

We protect your data from AI, at home and at work.

PrivacyPal is the filter between your data and every AI. Protection runs on the device, before a prompt or file reaches an AI provider: sensitive details are swapped for Privacy Twins, realistic stand-ins, so the AI still works and your real data never reaches the provider. One app protects you, your family and your business, so people and companies can say yes to AI with confidence.

Our story

Built from both sides of the desk

Our co-founders spent a decade shipping AI systems at scale, and a decade before that pioneering regulated data and financial systems that now form the standards in their industries. They watched CISOs become the blocker by default, teams build shadow-AI workarounds to ship, and "secure" tools degrade UX so badly the team routed around them. They built PrivacyPal so security teams could finally say yes.

Today we protect tens of millions of sensitive elements across financial services, healthcare, legal, telecom and B2B technology, from a 40-seat firm to organizations with thousands of employees. The same engine now protects families at home: parents choose the AI their kids use, and their kids' real identity never reaches the AI provider. The long-term vision: PrivacyPal as the AI-native security agent for the AI Operating System.
