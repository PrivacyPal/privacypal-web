For parents

The calm control room for AI in your home

Parent HQ is one warm screen that answers the three questions that matter: what AI are my kids using, is their identity safe, and how are they doing? And when you want to change something, it is one tap, applied in realtime.

Realtime control

Change your mind. Watch it apply.

Every control in Parent HQ takes effect on your kids' devices the moment you tap it. No sync delays, no "takes effect after restart," no IT degree required.

Approve & block AI tools

Green-light the AI you trust per child. Anything unapproved is blocked and redirected to a safe alternative, with a friendly explanation instead of a scary error.

Homework mode

School-day windows where AI helps with learning and skips the distractions. Set it once per child; adjust from your phone when plans change.

The never-share list

Your home address, school names, the little one's face, medical details. Put them on the list once and PrivacyPal keeps them out of every AI and every post.

Wellbeing Signal early access

A gentle mood trend per child, computed on their device from how they talk to AI. You see the weather, never the words, and get a nudge when the forecast shifts.

Sharing Guard early access

Risky social posts wait in a small queue for your OK: a jersey with the school logo, a caption with your street name. Everything else posts instantly.

Family Memory

Read what AI remembers about each child in plain language. Keep the useful, erase the rest forever with one tap. Erase means erase, even mid-conversation.

Parent HQ · in your pocket

A Sunday digest, not a Sunday chore

Most weeks you will not open Parent HQ at all. A two-minute digest lands on Sunday: what your kids explored, what got protected, and one thing worth talking about at dinner. Effortless means effortless.

Only interrupts when it matters

A blocked tool, a held post, a wellbeing shift. Otherwise, silence. No notification spam, ever.
Both parents, one HQ

Share access with a partner or co-parent. Same view, same controls, no arguing with the settings.
