Why you'll actually like it

It's not a leash. It's armor.

A secret identity, automatically

Every superhero has one. When you chat with AI, PrivacyPal swaps your real name, school, and address for a stand-in before the message leaves your device. The AI never knows the difference. Neither does anyone who hacks it.

Nothing gets slower or dumber

The swap takes about a third of a second. Your AI still writes, explains, and brainstorms exactly the same. Most people forget PrivacyPal is even running.

Your delete button actually deletes

AI companies remember what you tell them. With PrivacyPal, what AI remembers about you lives in your family's vault, and when you erase it, it is gone everywhere, forever. For real.
