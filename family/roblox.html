Why Roblox

Different place. Different risks.

With AI, the worry is what your kid shares with a machine. In Roblox, it is what they share with people. Most of those people are other kids having fun. Not all of them are.

Real people can reach them

Other players can send messages, friend requests and invites to talk somewhere else. That is the part of Roblox that needs a parent's say.

Your list knows your street

Roblox's own filters catch a lot, and they are good. Your never-share list adds what only your family knows: that your kid's school is Oakridge Elementary, or that your street is Pinecrest.

Settings, plus signals

Roblox gives parents real controls. Family adds the signals: when your kid tried to share their address, you see it as a count, and the message stays theirs.

What Family does in Roblox

Four protections. One quiet app.

Private details, held before they send

Messages, profile text and group posts are checked on the device before they leave. Anything on your never-share list, like a school, a street or a sibling's name, is held instead of sent. Your kid sees why, edits it, and sends again. Nothing is stored.

Five switches per child in Parent HQ: play, chat, friend requests, voice and spending. Play starts on. Everything social starts off. Your kid can ask for more, and you decide.

mechanism · per-child switches, enforced on the device
03

Roblox's own settings, set and kept

Chat inside games is controlled by Roblox's own account settings, which follow your kid to a phone, a console or a friend's house. Setup walks you through them, and we tell you if they change.

mechanism · guided setup in Roblox · alerts when a setting drifts

Questions parents ask

Fair questions. Straight answers.

How do I connect my kid's Roblox?
With your own parent account. You link it in Roblox, the way Roblox designed it, and PrivacyPal works on the family computer. Your kid's password and sign-in stay theirs.
Will Roblox still work normally?
Yes. Games load, join and play exactly as before. PrivacyPal looks after the social side (messages, profile, groups and friend requests) and leaves gameplay to Roblox. Game first, always, and Parent HQ shows you live coverage.
What do I see about my kid's Roblox?
Signals: which features are on, how many messages were held and why, and alerts. Their words stay theirs. It is the same line Family draws for AI, and your kid can see exactly what you see.
How does chat inside a game work?
You set it in Roblox's own parent settings, which apply on every device, and setup walks you through each one. Our in-game check, in early access, flags risky messages within seconds and lets your kid and you know right away.
How does it work on phones, tablets and consoles?
Through Roblox's own parent settings, which follow your kid's account to every device. Setup walks you through each one. PrivacyPal's message check runs on the family Mac today, with Windows next.
Does PrivacyPal change Roblox on my computer?
With your OK, PrivacyPal adjusts Roblox on the family Mac so it can check messages before they send. Gameplay stays exactly the same, and turning Roblox protection off puts Roblox back as it was.
What does Roblox protection cost?
It's included in PrivacyPal Family: $9.99 a month for up to 5 family members, and Founding Families pay half for their first year.
