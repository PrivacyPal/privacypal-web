Is this spyware on my kid?
No, and we built it to be the opposite. PrivacyPal Family never shows you transcripts of what your child says to an AI or writes on Roblox. You see signals: which tools they used, what identity details we protected, and wellbeing trends. Your child can open their own view and see exactly what you see. Kids who know the deal don't need to hide.
Does it work with Roblox?
Yes. Roblox gets its own switches in Parent HQ: play, chat, friend requests, voice and spending, with everything social starting off. On the family Mac, messages, profile text and group posts are checked before they send, and anything on your never-share list is held, never rewritten, with your kid told why. We never ask for their Roblox password and never show you their messages. Chat typed inside a game can't be held, so danger alerts for it are in early access. Roblox protection runs on the family Mac today; Windows is next. More about Roblox protection.
What exactly gets protected?
Real names, home address, phone numbers, school names, health details, and dozens more identity data types. The moment one is typed or uploaded to an AI, it is swapped with a safe stand-in (a Privacy Twin) before anything leaves the device. The AI's answer still makes sense. The real detail never left your house.
Will it slow down or break the AI tools my kids use?
No. Protection runs on the device and adds a fraction of a second. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Grok and Perplexity keep working exactly as before, on computers, phones and tablets. Most kids never notice it is running.
Which devices are covered today?
All of them: Mac and Windows computers, iPhone and iPad, and Android phones and tablets, for up to 5 family members on one plan. Roblox protection runs on the family Mac today; Windows is next.
