Questions parents ask

Fair questions. Straight answers.

Is this spyware on my kid?
No, and we built it to be the opposite. PrivacyPal Family never shows you transcripts of what your child says to an AI. You see signals: which tools they used, what identity details we protected, and wellbeing trends. If you turn on Deep Insights (early access, off by default), wellbeing analysis transiently reads the twin-protected version of a conversation, the same version the AI tool receives, then discards it: stored never, shown to no one. Your child can open their own view and see exactly what you see. Kids who know the deal don't need to hide.
Will it slow down or break the AI tools my kids use?
No. Protection runs on the device and adds around a third of a second. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Grok, and Perplexity keep working exactly as before, in the browser and in desktop apps, and Roblox games load and play as they always did. Most kids never notice it is running.
Does it cover Roblox?
Yes. Roblox gets its own switches in Parent HQ: play, chat, friend requests, voice and spending, with everything social starting off. On the family Mac, messages, profile text and group posts are checked before they send, and anything on your never-share list is held for your kid's edit, with the reason shown to them. Their password stays theirs, and you see signals, not their messages. Chat inside games follows Roblox's own settings, which setup walks you through, and danger alerts for it are in early access. See exactly what's covered.
What exactly gets protected?
Real names, home address, phone numbers, school names, health details, and dozens more identity data types. The moment one is typed or uploaded, it is swapped with a safe stand-in (a Privacy Twin) before anything leaves the device. The AI's answer still makes sense; the real detail never left your house.
What can I control from Parent HQ?
Approve or block AI tools per child, set homework mode hours, choose each child's stage (Explorer, Navigator, Pilot), decide what can never be shared, review what AI remembers about each child, and erase it with one tap. Changes apply to their devices in realtime.
What does "erase what AI remembers" actually do?
PrivacyPal keeps the memory your family builds with AI in a protected vault that only your family holds the keys to. Erasing destroys those keys, which makes the memory unreadable everywhere, permanently, even for work in flight. Erase means erase; it is a mechanism we test, not a settings toggle we hope providers honor.
Which devices are covered today?
Mac and Windows (browser and desktop AI apps) are covered now, for up to 5 family members. iPhone, iPad, and Android are rolling out to early access families, and wearables like smart glasses are on the roadmap. One plan covers all of it as it ships.
When do the wellbeing features arrive?
Wellbeing Signal and Answer Guard are in early access now. Founding Families get them first and help shape how they work. Everything on this page marked "early access" is in active testing with real families before general release.
