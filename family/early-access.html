Questions

Fair questions. Straight answers.

How does the invitation reach me?
By email. On iPhone and iPad the invitation installs PrivacyPal through TestFlight, Apple's free app for installing apps by invitation. On Android it comes through Google Play testing, straight from the Play Store. Open it on the device you want protected.
Which packs include phones and tablets?
The Family pack and the Max pack. A household on Family protects the kids' phones and tablets and puts Parent HQ on a parent's phone. On Max, a company's people get PrivacyPal on their phones and tablets, with seats and policy set by its admin. Both protect phones and tablets today, with installs by invitation, and Mac and Windows come with every pack.
Can I request for my household and my team?
Yes. Send one request for each. A household invitation comes with the Family pack's guardrails and Parent HQ; a team invitation comes with the Max pack your company runs. Your company can add Max to your PrivacyPal account under a license it provisions, and it stacks with your Family pack.
Do I pay inside the app?
Plans are set up on the web, so the phone apps have nothing to buy. The Family pack is $9.99 a month for the whole household, with a 5-day free trial. The Max pack is billed per user through your company. See pricing.
What about Mac and Windows?
They install directly, no invitation needed. Download PrivacyPal for Mac (Apple Silicon) or Windows, and sign in. Your phone and tablet invitation follows by email.
What happens to my answers?
They go to our team at hi@privacypal.ai and are used only to send your invitation. Nothing else lands in your inbox.
