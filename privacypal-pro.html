Questions

Personal, in plain answers

Is Personal really free?

Yes. Personal is free for individuals: no credit card, no trial clock and no expiry. Your account is live the moment your email is verified.

Which AI apps does Personal protect?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity chat, attachments included. Word and Excel files are protected in place, and PowerPoint files once you switch on PowerPoint protection in Settings › Privacy.

Where does the protection run?

On your device, before a prompt or file reaches an AI provider. Sensitive details are swapped for patent-pending Privacy Twins, realistic stand-ins, and swapped back to your real values in the answer, on your device.

Which computers does it run on?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 and later) on macOS 13 or newer, and Windows 11 PCs (64-bit). One download covers both: see every platform.

What do the Family and Max packs add?

The Family pack ($9.99 a month for up to 5 family members) adds guardrails for the AI your kids use and Parent HQ. The Max pack ($34 per user a month, less at volume) adds agents like Claude Code, Cowork and Codex, Connections, Privacy Agents, Private Memory, hosted Private MCP, admin and full history. Both switch on inside the same app.

Can my company add Max to my account?

Yes. Your company can invite you into its Max account under a license it provides. Max switches on in the same app, next to Personal, and it stacks with your Family pack if you have one.
